Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?

Open the app and choose Sign up. Use your email and a password to create an account.

How can I pay for my order?

At checkout, you can choose to pay by card or by bank account.

Can I get my order delivered?

Yes. Choose door delivery at checkout, or choose pickup if you want to collect your meal.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Choose Forgot password on the login screen and follow the steps to set a new password.

Where can I get the app?

The Bella Onoje app is available on the Play Store and the App Store.
